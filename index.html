♠ MASTER THE BASICS

Spades Rules & Strategy Guide

Everything you need to know to outplay opponents and become a grandmaster.

01

1. The Bidding Contract

At the start of each hand, players assess their cards and bid the exact number of tricks (books) they expect to win. In 2v2, partner bids are combined.

02

2. Nil & Blind Nil Strategy

Bid Nil to predict taking zero tricks for a 100-point reward. Trailing behind? Bid Blind Nil before looking at your hand for a massive 200-point comeback!

03

3. Beware the Sandbags

Taking more tricks than your bid awards 1 extra point per bag. However, accumulate 10 penalty bags and your team is slapped with a 100-point penalty!

04

4. Spades are Always Trumps

Spades outrank all other suits. You must follow the led suit if possible. You cannot lead with Spades until a player has 'broken' Spades by trumping a lead.
